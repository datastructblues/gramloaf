Support

Email contact.umutfidan@gmail.com. We usually reply within two business days.

I bought Pro but it's still locked

Open the Pro screen in the app (tap any Pro feature) and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought Pro with. If it still does not unlock, email us.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by the store, not by us:

  • iPhone: Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → Gramloaf.
  • Android: Play Store → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Gramloaf.

Pro stays active until the end of the period you already paid for. The lifetime option is a one-time purchase, so there is nothing to cancel.

I changed phones

Install Gramloaf on the new phone, open the Pro screen and tap Restore purchases with the same store account.

Gramloaf has no accounts and no server, so your recipes, starters and bakes move to a new phone only through your phone's own backup (iCloud backup on iPhone, Google backup on Android).

Reminders don't arrive

Allow notifications for Gramloaf in your phone's settings. Battery-saving modes can also delay reminders by a few minutes.

Are the times exact?

No. Fermentation times are a guide based on temperature and your starter. Every dough is different, so watch your dough, not only the clock.
