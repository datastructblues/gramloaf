Bake with confidence

Gramloaf is a calm, friendly baking companion for home bakers on iPhone and Android.

  • Dough calculator: baker's percentages and hydration turned into grams (or ounces), plus a yeast converter.
  • Sourdough with your starter: the starter's flour and water are counted in, so your hydration is the real one.
  • Baking plan with reminders: a step-by-step timeline with timers and gentle notifications.
  • Starter tracker and bake log: feeding reminders, plus notes, ratings and photos for every bake.

Free to start. Gramloaf Pro is optional and unlocks the sourdough tools, plans, starter tracker and bake log.

No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Your baking data stays on your phone.
