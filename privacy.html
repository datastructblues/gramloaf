Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026

Gramloaf ("the app", "we") is a sourdough and bread dough calculator and baking companion made by an independent developer. This policy explains what the app does with information. The short version: no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, and your baking data stays on your phone.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, sign-in or profiles.
  • No analytics or behaviour tracking.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • We do not sell or share your data, and we run no server that stores it.

Data stored on your device

The app keeps the following only on your device, in the app's private storage. We never receive it and the app never uploads it:

  • Settings and last-used values: for example units (grams or ounces), temperature unit, sound and haptics preferences, and the values you last entered in the calculators.
  • Recipe inputs you choose to keep or log with a bake.
  • Starters: the starters you create, their feeding history and reminder settings.
  • Bake log: your notes, ratings, tags, and photos you attach. Photos are copied into the app's private storage (resized copies and thumbnails). They are not uploaded anywhere.
  • Timeline: the bake plan you start and its timers.

You can delete a bake, a starter or a photo inside the app. Uninstalling the app removes its data from the device.

Device backups. Your operating system may include the app's data in your own device backup: Android Auto Backup (to your Google account) or an iCloud device backup. That is a feature of your phone and is controlled by your device settings; we do not have access to those backups. If you restore a backup on a new phone, your bakes and starters may come with it.

Purchases

The free tier needs no purchase. "Gramloaf Pro" can be bought as a one-time lifetime purchase, or as an auto-renewing monthly or yearly subscription, made through Apple's App Store or Google Play. Subscriptions renew automatically unless you cancel them at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; you manage or cancel them in your App Store or Google Play account settings. Apple or Google processes your payment; we never see your card or payment details.

We use RevenueCat to check and restore your purchase. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user id (a random identifier created by the app) and your purchase receipts / purchase history from the store, so it can tell the app whether Pro is unlocked (including whether a subscription is active). It is not linked to a name or email by us.

  • Apple privacy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
  • Google Play: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • RevenueCat: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/

Crash reports

The app does not send crash reports. If we add crash reporting in a future version, we will update this policy before it ships.

Notifications

Reminders for bake steps and starter feeding are local notifications scheduled on your device. They do not involve a server, and no data about them leaves your phone. You can turn notifications off at any time in your system settings.

Photos

When you attach a photo to a bake, the app opens the system photo picker. The app can see only the photos you choose; it does not get access to your photo library, and it asks for no photo-library permission. The chosen photos are copied into the app's private storage (see above). The app does not use the camera.

Children's privacy

Gramloaf is a general-audience cooking tool and is not directed at children under 13 (or the age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, write to contact.umutfidan@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Security

Data on your device is protected by your phone's own security (screen lock, app sandbox, device encryption). Purchase checks use encrypted connections (HTTPS). No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your device updated.

Your choices and rights

Because we hold no personal data about you on any server, there is usually nothing for us to access or delete. You control your data in the app and in your device settings. For purchase records, contact Apple or Google, or write to contact.umutfidan@gmail.com and we will help with RevenueCat. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights (for example under the GDPR or CCPA); write to contact.umutfidan@gmail.com to use them.

Disclaimer about baking guidance

Fermentation times and temperatures in the app are rough estimates, not guarantees. Use your own judgment and follow food-handling guidance from official sources.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy (for example when crash reporting ships, or a new feature handles data differently), we will update this page and the effective date, and for material changes mention it in the app's release notes.
